Software Engineering Internship @ Full Swing Golf

I worked at Full Swing Golf in the summer of 2024 as a Research & Development Intern (officially). This was my first real software engineering job. I mainly worked on a mini-golf game, as an alternative game to our main product, targeted towards kids. I also worked on a variety of other, smaller projects like a virtual golf shot debugger tool that injected a set payload of what the sensors see into the game to get a perfectly precise golf shot. I also wrote unit tests (which weren't as fun) and practiced my golf swing a LOT (which was fun!). Unfortunately, my project never made it to production, which was disappointing. Overall I learned a lot and I'm truly grateful to John and Bailey for giving me the opportunity and mentoring me.

Skills: C#, Python, Unity, Plastic SCM, Jira, Confluence, Golf
