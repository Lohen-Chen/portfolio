Graduation Cap

This graduation cap was me trying to combine my artistic skills with my technical skills. I chose a railway ring around the Purdue logo because of the Purdue student nickname, the Boilermakers. It is a reference to our school's history as a working-class engineering school with a world-class locomotive laboratory. I had seen a reel (credit to shebuildsrobots) of a similar graduation cap for UIUC. I had started working on this only three days before my graduation! Despite the time crunch, I still managed to get it done. The gear train kept binding, so I was sanding it with a glass file down to the last minute. In the end it worked, though! Now it lives in my dorm at Purdue.

Skills: Art, CAD
